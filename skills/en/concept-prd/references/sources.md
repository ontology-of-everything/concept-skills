| --- | --- |
| [Concept purposes](https://essenceofsoftware.com/posts/distillation/) | Distinguish application and concept purposes. |

[The local contract](spec-format.md) adapts [WYSIWID v1 sections 4 to 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14511v1#S4),
with labeled query/entry extensions. Fixed Markdown, unique file ownership, and acceptance traceability
are repository conventions. The OP explains a representative result, not the entire behavior.
