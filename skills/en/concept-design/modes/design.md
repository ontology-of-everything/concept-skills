# Design

1. Establish the beneficiary, application purpose, scope, current behavior, and required results.
   Inspect existing entries, state ownership, failures, and tests when available.
   Retain required security, timing, and reliability bounds.
2. Propose concepts and scenarios in the [decision record](../references/decision-records.md).
3. Apply all eight [qualification criteria](../references/concept-criteria.md).
   Write contracts and their execution constraints in the [specification notation](../references/spec-notation.md).
4. Design rules and check [composition and subsets](../references/composition.md).

Deliver concept contracts, rules, their diagram, exclusions, and open decisions.
Required responses MUST cover declared success and refusal cases.
Explain the model using the [reading version](../references/spec-reading.md) by default; expand precision as needed.
